Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what the Mithun Clips publisher (“the app”) does with data. The app is a private tool that only connects to TikTok accounts owned by the Mithun Clips channel.

1. Data we access

  • Basic profile information of our own TikTok account (such as the display name and account ID), to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access tokens that TikTok issues when we log in, so the app can upload videos.
  • Statistics of the videos we posted (such as views, likes and comments counts).

We do not collect data about other TikTok users, viewers or followers.

2. How we use it

Only to publish our own videos and to see how they perform, so we can make better videos.

3. Storage and security

All data stays on our own computer. Tokens are kept in a private file that is never shared or published. We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when we disconnect the app. You can also remove the app's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

5. Children

The app is not meant for children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
