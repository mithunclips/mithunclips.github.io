MITHUN CLIPS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what the Mithun Clips publisher (“the app”) does with data. The app is a private tool that only connects to TikTok and YouTube accounts owned by the Mithun Clips channel. It uses the TikTok developer APIs and the YouTube API Services.

1. Data we access

  • Basic profile information of our own TikTok account and YouTube channel (such as the display name and account ID), to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access tokens that TikTok and Google issue when we log in, so the app can upload videos.
  • Statistics of the videos we posted (such as views, likes and comments counts).

We do not collect data about other TikTok or YouTube users, viewers or followers.

2. How we use it

Only to publish our own videos and to see how they perform, so we can make better videos.

3. Storage and security

All data stays on our own computer. Tokens are kept in a private file that is never shared or published. We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are deleted when we disconnect the app. You can also remove the app's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. For YouTube, remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions (see also Google's security settings).

4a. Google and YouTube data

The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to upload and manage our own videos and read their statistics, is never sold or transferred, and is not used for advertising.

5. Children

The app is not meant for children and does not knowingly collect data from them.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
